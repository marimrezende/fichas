Nimbus · The Ink that Slows

Time that Breathes

How the world feels Reiki's presence

Long-Term Nimbus

The reality around Renji gently resists the world's rush. Digital clocks lose seconds without explanation. Machines that should have broken weeks ago still run. People who spend time with him report, without quite being able to say why, that they felt less urgency that day. Children settle. Conversations slow down, become more honest.

In the Chainwork, where rhythm is measured in biometrics and productivity per hour, this registers in the systems as a minor statistical anomaly. Not enough to trigger an alert. Just enough to be strange.

Immediate Nimbus · Brushstroke in the Air

When Renji casts magic, the air around him behaves like ink in water. Dark, fluid traces appear suspended: not smoke, not light, but something carrying the presence and intent of calligraphy. Those with Mage Sight see characters that almost form words before dissolving. Those without sense something else: the city's frantic rhythm simply… stops pressing for a moment.

The smell of wet sumi ink and old rice paper
Signature Nimbus · The Ink Trail

What he touches with magic is marked with invisible traces, perceptible only through Mage Sight, like brushstrokes of ink that dried in the air. Places where he worked carry a residual quality of stillness, like the silence after someone finishes writing something important.

Nimbus Tilt · The Brush's Stillness
+1 Composure
+1 Wits · at Gnosis 3

When the Nimbus surfaces, urgency around it dissolves. The world doesn't stop, it simply becomes bearable for a moment. The stillness taught by Fumiko spreads beyond him; when time loosens, you see more.

How a flare works

Tilt dice = half Gnosis, rounded up, so 1 now, 2 at Gnosis 3. A spell's flare uses its Potency as Nimbus strength and lasts its Reach in turns (minimum 1). Once per scene you can force a flare for 1 Mana: roll Gnosis, successes are the strength, visible even to people without Mage Sight. The Tilt lands on anyone whose Resolve is equal to or below the strength.
